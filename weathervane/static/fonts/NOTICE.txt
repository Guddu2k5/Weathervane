The poppins-*.woff files here are Latin-script subsets (Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended,
Latin Extended Additional, common punctuation and arrows) of the Light (300), Regular (400),
Medium (500) and Bold (700) weights, converted to WOFF. Text in other scripts falls back to
the system font. If you redistribute the project publicly, include the full OFL.txt from the
Poppins repository alongside these files.
